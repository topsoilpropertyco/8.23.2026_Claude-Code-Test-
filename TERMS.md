# Terms of Service — Sleep OS

_Last updated: 24 August 2026_

## What this is

Sleep OS is a personal, single-user, open-source project. It sends its operator
science-backed reminders about sleep and records their replies. It is not a
product, not a service, and not available for anyone else to sign up to.

## Use

The source is public so it can be read, learned from, and adapted. There is no
hosted service to use, no account to create, and no support to expect. Anyone
running their own copy does so at their own risk and under their own
credentials.

## Not medical advice

Sleep OS delivers summaries of published sleep research and generates
rule-based suggestions from the operator's own logged data. **It is not medical
advice, diagnosis, or treatment.** It is not a medical device and has not been
reviewed by any regulator. Nothing it sends should be used to diagnose, treat,
or manage any health condition. Consult a qualified professional for medical
concerns.

## Accuracy

The research summaries are drawn from published literature but are compressed
for brevity and may omit important context, caveats, or later findings.
Statistics are presented as motivational reframes, not as clinical guidance.
