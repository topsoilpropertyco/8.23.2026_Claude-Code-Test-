# BRIEF.md — one screen

**Last night's sleep.** iPhone, 390 × 844, opened at 7am, looked at for four
seconds and put down.

It answers two questions in that order: *how did I sleep,* and *how does that
compare to normal for me.* Everything else is available but subordinate.

## Content, in priority order

1. **88** — last night's sleep score.
2. **Where 88 sits** among 1,042 nights: mean 79.3, +0.92 SD, 81st percentile.
   This is not a secondary stat. It is half of the dominant element; the number
   is meaningless without it.
3. **The night itself** — 23:15 → 07:26, 7h 45m asleep, 94% efficient.
4. **Marginalia** — 1h 29m deep, 2h 07m REM, 4h 09m light, 27m awake, HRV 37ms,
   lowest HR 55, readiness 85, latency 2m 30s.
5. **Recent baseline** — T7 79.4, T30 79.2, T90 73.9.

## Rules

- Real values only. 7h 45m, not 8h. 79.3, not 80.
- One dominant element, declared per variant before building.
- No ring-plus-four-tiles. No legended hypnogram. See DESIGN.md §8.
- Bedtime and wake are content, not metadata — "23:15 → 07:26" is a more human
  fact than a duration, and no variant should bury it.

## Out of scope

Trends over time, the journal, the reminder cadence, settings, navigation
chrome. One screen, one night, one moment.
