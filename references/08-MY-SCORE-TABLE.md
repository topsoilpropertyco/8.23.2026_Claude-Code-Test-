# My own score table

**Every sleep score from 40 to 99, where it sits among my own nights, and the same three
letter grades the member table uses.** Generated by `bin/build-grades.mjs`.

> ⚠️ **PROVISIONAL — MODELLED, NOT COUNTED.** Telemetry unreadable in this environment (no SLEEPOS_DATA_KEY). Percentiles are a beta fit on the recorded mean, SD and count, not counted nights. Re-run where the key is present to replace every row with a census.

## The three curves

Identical to the member table, deliberately. Holding them fixed is what makes the two
screens comparable: one score, two populations, and the gap between the grades is the
thing worth looking at.

One property to know before reading the Standard column: **it fails ~60% of my nights and
always will.** Standard puts its pass line at the 60th percentile, and a pass line at the
60th percentile fails 60% of any dataset, forever, however well I sleep. It is a fact about
ranking, not about sleep. The Curved column is the one that moves.

An 88 is the **80.5th** percentile of my own nights and the **89.1th** of member nights. The gap is the whole story: I sleep better
than the member average, so my own history is the tougher field. Graded against Oura members
an 88 is a **A**; graded against my own nights it is a **A-**.
