03 · Operating rhythm

Entrepreneurial Time System + Top 3

Strategic Coach's three day types fit the friction barbell. Buffer days are for clearing low-leverage friction. Focus days are for the hard high-leverage work. Free days are for refueling. Focus OS reads the day type and changes its rules to match.

← swipe →

Free day

Barbell: off the bar · refuel
  • Off the computer. The only exception is pure learning.
  • Focus OS goes quiet: no pill, no nudges, no task list.
  • Opening a work tool counts as −$100/hr. It eats into recovery.
  • Foundation (sleep, sauna, family, play) is the win.

Buffer day

Barbell: frictionless pole
  • Clear friction debt: delete, delegate, automate.
  • Admin, prep, catch-up and delegation handoffs, all batched.
  • The engine suggests which recurring tasks to automate next.
  • A win means next Focus day starts with nothing in the way.

Focus day

Barbell: friction pole
  • Only $1,000/hr work. Top 3 comes first, before email or Slack.
  • The pill flags anything below +2 in red.
  • Low-leverage tasks get pushed to the next Buffer day automatically.
  • A win is all three of the Top 3 done.

The daily Top 3

Each morning, one 60-second check-in: three ideal moves, each tied to a goal. Do them first. A day with all three done counts as a win, and wins add up into a streak.

1 · done 9:48 · exampleSend the revised pricing proposalGoal: close Q4 anchor client
2 · done 11:20 · exampleHard call with the co-founder candidateGoal: build the team
3 · open · exampleWrite the Focus OS PRD outlineGoal: ship Focus OS v1
04 · Coaching prompts and tool library

The board's questions, at the moment they help

Each prompt is tied to a moment. The app shows one at the right time, not as a wall of quotes.

Who, not how?Shown when you start a low-leverage task you've done before. (Dan Sullivan)
What's the one thing that makes everything else easier or unnecessary?Shown at the morning Top 3 check-in. (Gary Keller)
What am I avoiding?Shown when you've drifted for 10 minutes on a Focus day.
What would this look like if it were easy?Shown on friction-debt tasks. (Tim Ferriss)
Can this be deleted?Shown when a task has been carried over three times.
What's the harder version?Shown when high-leverage work feels easy. This is the moat-leak check.

Tool library: the frictionless pole's toolbox

Your own list, and it grows. Each friction-debt task gets matched to the first step that applies.

← swipe →

1Delete

Say-no templates, unsubscribe sweeps, meeting declines, and "not this quarter" lists.

2Delegate

Your Who list: assistant, team, contractors. Handoff templates and a checklist for what "done" means.

3Automate

Claude agents, Zapier or Make flows, email templates, and Sleep OS–style scheduled nudges.

4Do fast

Batch it into a Buffer day, set a timer, and stop at good enough.

06 · Build options

Where Focus OS lives on your screen

The always-on pill you described is the signature piece. It floats above every window, reads your running Toggl timer, and shows the tier and dollar rate live. Click it to open the full dashboard.

⚠ DECEPTIVEInbox·real −$990/hr·0:18·Who, not how?
Green on paper, so the pill turns yellow and shows the prompt that applies.
−2 NEGATIVENo timer running·11 min drift·What are you avoiding?
No timer running on a Focus day means drift. One tap to start the Top 3 item or log why you skipped it.
